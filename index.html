Top AI Tools for Teachers in 2026

1. ChatGPT

ChatGPT is one of the most powerful AI tools for teachers. It can create lesson plans, worksheets, classroom activities and project ideas.

2. MagicSchool AI

MagicSchool AI is built specifically for educators and helps generate rubrics, assignments, parent communication templates and educational resources.

3. Canva AI

Canva AI allows teachers to create presentations, posters, classroom graphics and visual learning materials.

4. Quizizz AI

An excellent AI quiz generator for teachers that helps create engaging assessments and track student performance.

5. Khanmigo

Provides AI tutoring support and personalized assistance for students and educators.

6. Grammarly

Improves teacher communication, assignment feedback and classroom documentation.

7. Curipod

Creates interactive lessons, polls and presentations that improve student participation.
